# KinetikWeather 1.1.0

**A new look.** A big temperature over a soft wash of the sky's colour, a row of details (feels like, wind with its direction, humidity, UV index), the next hours as a smooth temperature curve with rain bars behind it, and each section on its own rounded panel. Icons have soft gradients.

**Several places at once.** Right-click → Places → Add a place… (up to eight). Show just the main place, the main place in full with the others as compact rows below it (the new default), or every place as a compact row. Each row has the place's local time when it's in another time zone, its conditions, the next 12 hours as a little line, and the day's range. Click a row, or use Places → Make it the main place, to show that place in full. Move up, Move down and Remove are there too.

**Terminal themes.** Adds three terminal themes, the same green, amber and yellow phosphor colours as Kinetik's dashboard and Kinetik Screen: **Terminal**, **Amber terminal** and **Yellow terminal**. They draw in a monospace font (Cascadia Mono, or Consolas) unless you've picked a font, and their divider lines glow in the theme's colour. Pick one from the theme list, or from Kinetik Hub's Theme for all. The weather icons become line drawings in the theme's colour.

The main place's forecast is still saved in `forecast.json`; the others go in `places\`, listed in `places.tsv`, which Kinetik Screen 1.4.0 reads.
